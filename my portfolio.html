Superstore sales dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard showing where sales turn into profit, and where they don't.

Question

Which regions, categories, and products make money, and which ones lose it?

What I did

Took 1,993 order lines (969 orders, calendar year 2014), added calculated columns for revenue and profit, and built 11 pivot tables. Combined them into a dashboard with 6 charts and a slicer so viewers can filter the numbers themselves.

What I found

  • Sales totaled $484.2K with $49.5K profit, a 10.2% margin.
  • Central region earned a 0.5% margin, against 13.6% in the West.
  • Furniture had a 3.5% margin. Office Supplies was the strongest at 14.9%.
  • Tables lost $3.1K overall. Texas lost $9.1K.
  • Discounted items brought in $269.3K in sales but lost $9.1K, while full-price items earned $58.6K profit.

Adidas sales performance capstone

A $899.9M sales analysis with recommendations for management.

Question

Which products, retailers, channels, and regions drive Adidas sales and profit, and where should the company focus?

What I did

Analyzed 2020 to 2021 sales in Excel across four sheets: an executive summary with KPIs, then product, retailer and channel, and regional breakdowns. Ended with five strategic recommendations.

What I found

  • Total revenue was $899.9M with a 36.9% operating margin.
  • Men's Street Footwear led sales at $208.8M (23.2% share).
  • Online is the smallest channel by revenue (27.5%) but sells the most units and earns the highest margin (39.0%).
  • The West region is largest ($269.9M), but the South earns the best margin at 42.3%.
  • West Gear and Foot Locker together account for over 51% of retailer revenue.
  • Q3 and Q4 of 2021 had large seasonal spikes, so I recommended stocking up about six weeks earlier.

Ikeja phone repair shop dashboard

A dashboard for a repair shop's August to September sales, built from messy raw data.

Question

Which repairs, regions, and phone models bring in the most money, and how many jobs actually get completed?

What I did

Organized 30 repair records into pivot tables by region, repair type, phone model, and job status, then built a one-page dashboard with charts and key insights. While checking the results, I found that inconsistent spelling and extra spaces (like "Lagos" and "Lagos ") split the same category into separate rows.

What I found

  • Total repair value was ₦19.24M across 30 jobs.
  • Battery repairs earned the most (₦6.91M), followed by screens (₦5.90M).
  • 22 of 30 jobs (73%) were completed successfully. 5 were returning jobs and 3 were declined.
  • Once entries were cleaned and merged, Lagos led on revenue at ₦6.09M, ahead of Ogun at ₦5.02M.

Retail transactions: Excel formulas

A hands-on exercise in formulas, validation, and formatting.

Question

How can a raw transaction list be turned into something clean, searchable, and easy to check?

What I did

Worked with 11 retail transactions from 2023. Extracted parts of text with LEFT, MID, RIGHT, and LEN, joined fields into a full description with CONCATENATE, and flagged high-value sales with an IF formula. Added a drop-down list for product category and conditional formatting on totals.

What I found

  • Total sales were 2,860 across three categories.
  • Clothing made up 65% of sales (1,850), ahead of Electronics (730) and Beauty (280).
  • No transaction was above the 1,000 high-value threshold.
  • IF
  • LEFT / MID / RIGHT
  • CONCATENATE

  • Conditional formatting
